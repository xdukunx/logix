LOGIX

Design Foundation

The visual system for a privacy-first lab access log. Friendly-but-serious research tooling — trustworthy, calm, information-dense, and honest about what it does and doesn't track.

Personality

Reassuring, not surveilling. Every screen makes it clear who, how, and when — with privacy shown in the open, never buried. Precise like a lab instrument; warm like a good colleague.
